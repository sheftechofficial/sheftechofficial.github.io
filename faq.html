Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, processes, and how we can help grow your business.

What type of Shopify mentorship do you offer?+

We offer 1-on-1 mentorship where you work directly with an expert on your own store. Sessions cover store setup, ads, sales funnels and marketing, and you can message your mentor on WhatsApp between sessions.

How do I get started with your eCommerce setup service?+

Send us a message on the contact page or call us. Tell us about your business and goals, we agree on the scope and price, and then we start building.

Do you provide website maintenance and ongoing support?+

Yes. After your site goes live we can keep helping with updates, fixes and small changes. Contact us to talk through what you need.

How long does a store setup take?+

It depends on the size of your store and how quickly we get your content and products. A standard store usually takes from a few days to a couple of weeks. We’ll give you a clear timeline before we start.

What makes Sheftechofficial different from other digital agencies?+

You get design, ads, SEO and mentorship from one team that explains things in plain language. We care about results for your business, not just a nice-looking website.

Do you offer payment plans for your services?+

For larger projects we can talk about splitting the payment into stages. Reach out and we’ll find an arrangement that works.

Can you help with existing websites that need improvement?+

Absolutely. We can review your current site and fix speed, design, SEO and conversion problems without starting from scratch.

What’s included in your social media marketing service?+

Content planning, posting and scheduling, community engagement and paid ads on platforms like Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, with regular updates on how your accounts are doing.

Do you provide training on how to manage my website after completion?+

Yes. We walk you through managing products, orders and content so you can run the site confidently on your own.

What if I’m not satisfied with the results?+

Tell us right away. We’ll go back over the agreed scope together and keep working until it’s put right.
